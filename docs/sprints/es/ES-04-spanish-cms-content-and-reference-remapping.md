# ES-04 — Spanish CMS content and reference remapping

**Prerequisites:** ES-03  
**Owners:** Spanish translator, subject-matter reviewer, legal reviewer, content operations  
**Human gate:** qualified Spanish editorial approval; legal approval where required

## Goal

Produce complete, reviewed Spanish CMS partners for every release-scope English source.

## Work

- Translate the eight localized content types using the glossary and Spanish style guide.
- Remap internal references only to approved Spanish partners; leave original-language evidence, technical identifiers, URLs, APIs, and signed material intact.
- Create Arabic-independent review logs: translation, subject matter, legal, and publish approval.
- Run completeness reports for collections, detail routes, related content, geo services, and all switcher targets.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every Spanish collection/detail page has published approved Spanish content for its entire release scope.
- No Spanish document points to an English document where an approved Spanish partner is required.
- No exact quotation, client proof, metric, ID, or evidence file is translated without approval.

## Rollback

Unpublish incomplete Spanish documents and remove their translation-target links; keep source-language records and review history intact.

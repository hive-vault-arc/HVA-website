# Website Design Lessons Ledger

Status: active
Purpose: record verified corrections so future website work does not repeat visual drift.

## How To Use This Ledger

- Add a lesson after a confirmed QA issue, explicit direction from Ali, or repeated implementation defect.
- Record the problem, durable rule, prevention check, and affected surface.
- Mark a lesson `Codified` after it is represented in `AGENTS.md`, `DESIGN.md`, an audit rule, a test, or a screenshot gate.
- Keep one-off page details in the active sprint rather than turning every preference into a permanent rule.

## Lessons

| ID | Date | Trigger and learning | Durable rule | Prevention | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WEB-001 | 2026-08-13 | Page-specific redesigns introduced different navies, golds, grays, blue accents, and warm surfaces. The site stopped reading as one company. | Every visible UI color must map to the exact palette in `DESIGN.md`. Media and framed client/product proof are the only content-color exceptions. | Run `npm run audit:brand`; reject new literals outside approved tokens. | Codified |
| WEB-002 | 2026-08-13 | The standalone mark was added beside many section labels, weakening hierarchy and making the logo feel decorative. | Use one nav logo, one footer lockup, and at most one additional intentional brand moment on a long page. Routine section labels do not receive a logo. | Count brand marks during route screenshot QA. | Codified |
| WEB-003 | 2026-08-13 | Legacy numbered WebP logos and different light/dark treatments created inconsistent identity across routes. | Use canonical SVG marks, wordmarks, and responsive lockups from `.branding/logos/`; do not use numbered logo WebPs in new work. | Brand audit reports legacy logo references. | Codified |
| WEB-004 | 2026-08-13 | ARC became a dark cinematic microsite while Capabilities, Industries, About, and Home followed a light editorial language. | All page families share one light-first shell, type system, navigation, surface rhythm, and component grammar. Dark treatment is an inset emphasis, not a separate page theme. | Compare migrated route screenshots side by side before acceptance. | Codified |
| WEB-005 | 2026-08-13 | Long manifesto titles and very large serif type made sections feel inconsistent and delayed comprehension. | Page H1s normally use 6-10 words and fit in two desktop lines or three mobile lines. Use hierarchy before size. | Screenshot at 320px and 1440px; reject cropping or excess line count. | Codified |
| WEB-006 | 2026-08-13 | Decorative AI images, repeated capability art, and numerous image elements made pages noisy and generic. | Every image must provide proof, people, context, explanation, or publication identity. Remove imagery without a clear job. | Record the job for each retained image in the migration review. | Codified |
| WEB-007 | 2026-08-13 | Full-bleed dark bands on otherwise light pages felt pasted in and touched viewport edges abruptly. | Keep dark content modules inside shared page gutters with breathing room. The footer is the routine full-bleed exception. | Check 16px minimum phone edge space and 24-48px desktop edge space. | Codified |
| WEB-008 | 2026-08-13 | Client and generated blue imagery influenced surrounding corporate UI, creating an unofficial electric-blue brand. | Client/product colors stay inside proof frames. Corporate UI remains navy, amber, white, and approved neutrals. | Inspect frames and surrounding captions/controls separately. | Codified |
| WEB-009 | 2026-08-13 | Several distinct gold values were used interchangeably without purpose. | `#E8A838` is the website's visible brand gold. Never introduce a dark-gold text substitute; use navy or body gray when gold lacks sufficient contrast for normal copy. | Audit literal usage and review token role during component migration. | Codified |
| WEB-010 | 2026-08-13 | The existing `DESIGN.md` allowed black gradients, blue accents, glass effects, and colors that contradicted current branding. | Current approved brand files and explicit owner decisions supersede stale generated design guidance. | Read `AGENTS.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and this ledger before visual edits. | Codified |
| WEB-011 | 2026-08-13 | Wide generated scenes produced distant subjects, soft detail, and destructive crops when forced into website frames. | Generate for a declared final ratio with one close focal subject, safe zones, and enough source resolution for the target frame. | Reject assets that need extreme cropping; verify phone, tablet, and desktop crops before use. | Codified |
| WEB-012 | 2026-08-13 | Compact symbol-only navigation did not communicate the company name or positioning clearly, especially on the ARC hero treatment. | Every site navbar uses the approved full lockup with its positioning line; select the navy or white asset for the actual surface and preserve a legible rendered height. | Test the shared navbar on light pages, the unscrolled ARC hero, scrolled state, and the mobile menu. | Codified |
| WEB-013 | 2026-08-14 | Long headings and clause-heavy descriptions broke layouts and made technical ideas harder to scan. | Use short, active sentences and familiar words. Keep the voice technical, direct, and inviting; one idea per sentence. | Review copy length during screenshot QA and reject broken words, headings over three mobile lines, or supporting sentences that carry several ideas. | Codified |
| WEB-014 | 2026-08-14 | Headings inside capped frames used viewport-width sizing, so they grew and rewrapped when the browser widened even though their columns did not. | Fluid typography inside a max-width shell scales from that shell with `cqw`, or changes at explicit breakpoints. Never pair capped columns with `vw`/`vh` font sizing. | Run `npm run audit:responsive` and compare every affected page at both 1440px and 1920px. | Codified |
| WEB-015 | 2026-08-14 | Repeating gold across small labels on light surfaces reduced contrast and made the brand accent feel routine. | Use navy or body gray for light-surface labels. Keep gold for graphic structure, icons, sequence markers, dark-surface text, and one large editorial phrase per major section. | Audit computed text colors on the homepage and confirm gold remains concentrated in accents rather than routine copy. | Codified |
| WEB-016 | 2026-08-14 | Dividers between every homepage section made the page feel assembled from disconnected modules. | Use spacing and surface changes for most transitions. Keep borders only at meaningful hierarchy changes such as proof strips, editorial canvas changes, FAQ entry, and the footer. | Review the full-page rhythm and reject repeated rules between adjacent light sections. | Codified |
| WEB-017 | 2026-08-15 | Related capability and industry pages used separate hero scales and large empty introduction blocks, so the website looked inconsistent even when colors and fonts matched. | Related page families share the same hero scale, gutters, reading measures, and vertical rhythm. Give each page identity through its information structure and media composition, not larger type or more empty space. | Compare sibling routes together at 390px, 820px, 1440px, and 1920px; reject desktop H1s above two lines and mobile H1s above three. | Codified |
| WEB-018 | 2026-08-16 | Vertical wrapper padding and a strong translucent navbar shadow exposed a second gray bar behind the navigation surface. | The fixed navbar reads as one surface: its wrapper stays transparent and flush to the shell, with no resting shadow and only a subtle shadow after scrolling. | Compare the header and shell rectangles at rest and after scroll; their heights must match and the resting shell must have no box shadow. | Active |
| WEB-019 | 2026-08-16 | Search-intent landing pages appeared as prominent homepage cards and made the editorial section feel like visible SEO scaffolding. | Keep SEO routes discoverable through metadata and the sitemap, but use homepage modules for visitor decisions, evidence, or preparation rather than search-directory links. | Inspect homepage editorial cards and confirm they do not link to local SEO landing pages. | Active |

## Promotion Rule

Promote a lesson to `AGENTS.md` when it is repeated, high-risk, likely to be forgotten, or applies to every route. Promote a lesson to an automated check when it can be detected deterministically.

# Spanish style guide — Spain

## Voice

- Use formal European Spanish (`usted` where direct address is required).
- Keep the tone direct, practical, technically credible, and suitable for IT consulting and software delivery.
- Prefer clear verbs and short active sentences over inflated consulting language.
- Use familiar Spain-oriented technical vocabulary without regional slang.

## Writing rules

- Use sentence case for headings, buttons, navigation, and labels.
- Keep CTA labels concise and action-led: *Descubrir*, *Ver el programa*, *Hablar con el equipo*, *Reservar una llamada*.
- Preserve product names, company names, APIs, code, technology trademarks, URLs, IDs, dates, metrics, and exact client quotations.
- Use Spanish punctuation, accents, and opening punctuation where natural. Avoid unnecessary exclamation marks in CTAs.
- Format dates, numbers, currencies, and percentages with `Intl` using `es-ES`; do not hardcode month names or separators.

## Review

- A Spain-based technical/brand reviewer approves commercial, product, metadata, and CTA copy.
- A legal reviewer approves privacy, legal notices, contractual wording, and client-evidence context.
- AI output is a draft only. It cannot set `translationStatus: 'approved'` or publish content.

## Protected terms

Do not translate company names, person names, client names, product names, URLs, file names,
technology trademarks, signed quotations, metrics, dates, identifiers, source references, or
evidence files. Translate surrounding context, captions, labels, and explanations only after the
required editorial and legal review.

# Page-role and overlap decisions

Decision date: 2026-09-13. These decisions prevent search-intent overlap; they do not approve unverified public claims.

| Page group | Distinct user need | Distinct intent | Unique utility/evidence | Desired next action | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/capabilities` and six `/capabilities/[slug]` pages | Choose a capability vs evaluate one capability deeply | Capability overview vs capability-specific evaluation | Index provides comparison; detail pages provide scope, constraints, process, proof, and dependencies | Open the best-fit capability, then contact | Keep; index must remain a chooser and details must avoid repeated openings |
| `/ai-agents-tangier` and `/ai-agents-morocco` | Find a local Tangier provider vs evaluate national operating fit | Local commercial vs national commercial | Tangier page provides local discovery path; Morocco page owns broader workflow, governance, and market context | Request discovery | Keep only while the Tangier page retains genuine local utility and the Morocco page remains broader |
| `/digital-services-tangier` and specific service pages | Decide which intervention is needed vs evaluate that intervention | Local service selector vs service purchase | Selector uses simplify/configure/integrate/automate/build decision logic; detail pages provide evidence and delivery depth | Select service then contact | Keep; never turn the selector into duplicated capability copy |
| `/custom-software-morocco` and `/capabilities/software-engineering` | Commission custom work in Morocco vs evaluate engineering capability | Market purchase intent vs capability evaluation | Commercial page owns local buying, ownership, and build-vs-buy; capability page owns engineering method and quality | Contact vs inspect engineering depth | Keep with reciprocal contextual links |
| `/it-consulting-tangier` and `/capabilities/technology-consulting` | Find local consulting in Tangier vs understand consulting method | Local commercial vs capability evaluation | Local page owns Tangier engagement context; capability page owns architecture, decision records, and delivery governance | Contact vs evaluate method | Keep with non-duplicated titles and openings |
| `/arc` and `/capabilities/solution-programs` | Understand the operating philosophy vs choose a packaged transformation path | Brand/method trust vs program evaluation | ARC explains the continuous accountability model; programs explain selectable delivery structures | Explore programs or contact | Keep; do not make ARC a second program catalogue |
| Blog, Perspectives, and Research Reports | Read an article, understand a company viewpoint, or inspect evidence | Educational/news vs interpretation vs evidence validation | Blog/news explains developments; Perspectives owns reasoned positions; Research owns methodology, data, limitations, and tables | Continue to relevant capability/evidence/contact | Keep; a report cannot be a short opinion renamed for SEO |

If a page loses the distinct need, intent, utility, evidence, or next action recorded here, its content must be merged into the stronger page and the weaker URL permanently redirected. Canonical tags are not a substitute for consolidation.

# Brand Copy Lint Checklist

Use this checklist whenever editing externally visible copy, metadata, schema, FAQs, or AI-consumed files.

## Canonical Descriptor

- Required baseline descriptor:
  - `Hive Vault Arc (H.V.A) is a technology consulting and digital transformation firm.`
- Required execution line:
  - `We advise, engineer, build, ship, and maintain intelligent systems across AI, automation, custom software, IT modernization, cloud infrastructure, and data services.`

## Forbidden Identity Framing

- Do not present H.V.A as:
  - `agency-first`
  - `just an AI agency`
  - `chatbot shop`
  - `tool builder only`

## Allowed Search-Intent Coverage

- These phrases can be used only for search-intent coverage, not as the primary identity headline:
  - `AI agency Morocco`
  - `software agency Tangier`
  - `automation agency Morocco`

## Page-Specific Tone Constraints

- Home:
  - Lead with consulting authority and transformation outcomes.
  - Keep AI relevance as one capability pillar, not the whole identity.
- Services:
  - Emphasize lifecycle model: advisory, architecture, delivery, modernization, managed evolution.
- About:
  - Emphasize founder-led ownership, strategic problem-solving, and long-term partnership posture.
- Portfolio / Case Studies:
  - Describe business challenge, strategy, execution architecture, and operating impact.
- Products & Systems:
  - Frame offerings as consulting-led system programs, not standalone product claims.
- Blog:
  - Prioritize strategy + execution insights and practical decision-maker utility.
- Contact:
  - Position as strategic discovery and transformation scoping.

## SEO + AI Surface Checks

- Ensure metadata, schema, and `public/llms.txt` use consistent identity framing.
- Keep commercial model consistent:
  - `Pricing is discussed after discovery.`
- Ensure FAQs reinforce consulting + execution + long-term maintenance positioning.

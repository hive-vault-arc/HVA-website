Initial prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide landscape website image for the Build chapter of a business technology studio. Create a believable, tactile software-engineering work moment at close range: one adult engineer's hand actively testing a custom mobile app prototype on a real phone docked beside a laptop with a code editor in soft focus. The physical phone and testing gesture are the unmistakable subject, with a real USB cable and brushed metal workspace; intimate editorial photography, slight everyday imperfections, natural shallow depth of field, restrained directional light. The screens may show simple unlabeled interface blocks and defocused code texture, never readable words or figures. Deep navy and charcoal environment with neutral white highlights and one restrained warm amber reflection, matching a serious but approachable software studio. Compose so a narrow vertical crop of the central 60% still retains the hand and phone, and a horizontal 16:10 crop also works. No people’s faces, no logos, no readable text, no dashboards or charts, no floating interface layers, no neon lines, no cyan, teal or purple, no sci-fi glow, no stock-photo clutter, no printed photos.

The initial phone screen resembled a travel app. The selected master came from this edit prompt:

> Precise-object-edit. Edit only the visible phone screen in this photo. Replace the landscape/travel photograph with a believable, simple business workflow app prototype: neutral off-white and slate interface blocks, a short vertical list of unlabeled tasks or records, one restrained amber status indicator, no legible words, letters, numbers, charts, logos or photos. Keep the phone, hand, cable, laptop, desk, camera angle, lighting, crop and all surrounding pixels as close to the original as possible.

## Shared direction

- Capabilities and ARC imagery shows practical IT consulting, planning, software, infrastructure, and managed operations.
- Industry imagery shows the sector itself and contains no generated people.
- Generated screens are avoided or reduced to non-readable status shapes.
- No logos, public claims, page covers, Sanity proof, or locked Real Estate imagery are changed.

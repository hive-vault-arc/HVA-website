# Semantic Image Masters — Revision 03

Status: approved by Ali on 2026-08-24.

These source PNGs are the approved software-focused semantic image family. They
ship at their generated native size. Responsive WebP delivery files are cropped
or downscaled only; the exporter refuses to enlarge a source.

## Shared rules

- ARC, capability, and Home imagery explains a specific software-delivery or IT-consulting job.
- Generated people are anonymous adult men shown through hands or back/side views.
- Industry imagery contains no generated people and shows sector operations.
- Screens use restrained code, product, status, or operational evidence rather than becoming dashboard walls.
- Cloud ecosystem brands remain accessible vectors outside raster artwork.
- Heroes, the Home route block, Sanity proof, solution-program proof, and the Real Estate image remain locked.

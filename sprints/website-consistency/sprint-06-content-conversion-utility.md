# Sprint 06: Content, Conversion, And Utility Families

## Goal

Complete the visual migration for editorial, proof, conversion, local landing, and utility routes.

## Work

- Standardize insights, blog, research, news, perspectives, and article layouts.
- Standardize case-study indexes and detail proof structure.
- Standardize contact, forms, local landing pages, FAQs, and trust modules.
- Standardize privacy, legal, links, loading, error, and not-found states.
- Reduce generic AI imagery and prevent SEO landing pages from inventing visual systems.
- Preserve schema, metadata, canonical links, CMS content, and locale behavior.

## Acceptance

- Editorial reading measure and hierarchy are consistent across all article sources.
- Forms are readable, keyboard-accessible, and full-width on mobile.
- Case-study proof is framed and client colors do not leak into site UI.
- Utility and legal pages remain quiet and readable.
- Every family passes the page-family acceptance matrix.
